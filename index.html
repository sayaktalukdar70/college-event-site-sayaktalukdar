    About the department

    The Department of Computer Science & Engineering (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning) at the Institute of Engineering & Management, Kolkata, teaches students to build intelligent systems. Through hackathons, workshops, expert lectures and cultural evenings, the department gives students a place to apply classroom learning and meet people who work in the field.
